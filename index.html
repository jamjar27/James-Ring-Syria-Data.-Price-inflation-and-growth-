Real GDP since December 2024

Annual only: Syria publishes no monthly or quarterly GDP. 2024 and 2025 are World Bank estimates (0.8% and 3.6%); 2026 onward are three scenarios. Dashed lines are projections.

Pessimistic: 8% in 2026 (World Bank low end), then 3%, 2%, 2%. Reasonable: 10% in 2026 (World Bank high end and IMF “over 10%”), then 7%, 5.5%, 5%. High: 12.8% in 2026 (Finance Ministry, 17 Nov 2026), then 10%, 9%, 8.5% (government target of more than 8% a year to 2029). Years after 2026 are assumptions, not official forecasts.

Sources: Syrian Center for Policy Research monthly CPI bulletins (Dec 2024 – Sep 2026); World Bank Macro Poverty Outlook (Oct 2026); IMF staff statement (Aug 2026); Syrian Ministry of Finance (Nov 2026). Urban average = Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Hama, Lattakia, Tartous; rural average = the other eight governorates (simple averages).
